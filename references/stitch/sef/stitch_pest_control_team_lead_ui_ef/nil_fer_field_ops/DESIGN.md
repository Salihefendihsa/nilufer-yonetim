## Brand & Style

The design system balances technical field utility with biological precision, warmth, and quiet institutional authority. Geared specifically towards mobile operational workflows for pest control and chemical treatment team leaders, the UI eliminates visual ambiguity under harsh sunlight, fast-paced vehicle transit, and demanding on-site scheduling environments.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Operational Ergonomics** with **Crisp Organic Modernism**:
- **Field Authority & Trust:** Grounded deep forest greens and botanical mids project reliability, safety compliance, and environmental stewardship.
- **Ergonomic Speed:** Chunky touch targets, explicit status badges, and rapid visual hierarchy enable immediate decision-making within one-handed thumb range.
- **Airy Precision:** Generous whitespaces, crisp 1px borders, and soft organic rounded corners remove the industrial clunkiness typical of legacy field service software, instilling calmness and team mastery.

## Colors

The palette establishes high chromatic readability against a light, clean environmental canvas. It limits decorative hues to operational semantics.

### Core Tokens
- **Primary Green (`#3D8A4E`):** The primary driver for key actions, active navigation items, progress milestones, and prominent action points.
- **Dark Green (`#2F5233`):** Anchor tone for drawer headers, high-emphasis operational headers, cards with inverted contrast, and commanding text accents.
- **Light Green Tint (`#F1F8F2`):** Primary structural tint; used for badge backgrounds, active list states, and subtle container highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas / Background (`#F4F7F4`):** Soft, tinted off-white background that prevents glare and softens high-contrast screens outdoors.
- **Surface / Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white container for tactical cards, sheets, dialogs, and popovers.
- **Border Default (`#E3E8E3`):** Neutral organic boundary, ensuring subtle separation without heavy visual lines.

### Text & Communication
- **Text Primary (`#16211A`):** Deep botanical charcoal for headers and high-priority labels; WCAG AAA compliant against white surfaces.
- **Text Secondary (`#5A6B5E`):** Balanced mid-tone for auxiliary labels, timestamps, and secondary operational details.
- **Text Faint (`#8B9A8E`):** Tertiary quiet grey-green for field captions, placeholders, and inactive icons.

### Semantic Tones
- **Success (`#15803D`):** Completed treatments, certified zones, fully stocked kits.
- **Warning (`#B57F13`):** Impending appointments, chemical replenishment notices, weather risks.
- **Danger (`#C0392B`):** Overdue tasks, critical bio-hazard alerts, re-treatment flags.
- **Info (`#1F6FA8`):** Customer notes, route updates, equipment diagnostics.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** powers the entire system. Its wide geometric foundation, open counters, and humanistic touch provide exceptional legibility on compact handheld screens even through dust or sunlight glare.

- **Headlines:** Set with tight tracking to maximize screen economy on mobile devices without losing punch.
- **Labels & Numbers:** Bolded, high-contrast tracking for metrics and equipment counts to enable glanceable reading in the field.
- **Body:** Standard neutral letter spacing with relaxed line heights for easy reading of detailed chemical specs and customer addresses.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 4px/8px modular scale structures the mobile interface.

- **Horizontal Margins:** Consistent `16px` (`space-md`) screen padding on mobile, expanding to `24px` on compact tablet breakpoints.
- **Vertical Rhythm:**
  - Card internal padding is uniformly `16px` (`space-md`).
  - Stacking cards maintain a `12px` (`space-sm`) margin to keep information cohesive.
  - Section blocks separate by `24px` to `32px` to prevent cognitive fatigue during route reviews.
- **Touch Targets:** Interactive areas strictly conform to a minimum size of `44x44px`, with standard field buttons hitting `48px` or `52px` in height to accommodate gloved or rapid tap interactions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid architecture of crisp hairline borders (`1px solid #E3E8E3`) combined with delicate, organic ambient shadows tinted with dark botanical undertones.

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Standard screens and neutral backgrounds (`#F4F7F4`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):**
  - Border: `1px solid #E3E8E3`
  - Shadow: `0px 2px 8px -1px rgba(22, 33, 26, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px -0.5px rgba(22, 33, 26, 0.03)`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards, Dropdowns & Modals):**
  - Border: `1px solid #D8E0D9`
  - Shadow: `0px 8px 20px -4px rgba(22, 33, 26, 0.08), 0px 3px 6px -2px rgba(22, 33, 26, 0.04)`
- **Level 3 (Raised Floating Action Button & Overlays):**
  - Shadow: `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(61, 138, 78, 0.35), 0px 4px 10px -2px rgba(47, 82, 51, 0.12)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces a unified `rounded-2xl` contour philosophy for primary surfaces:

- **Cards & Sheets:** Standardized at `16px`–`18px` border-radius to foster an approachable, non-industrial aesthetic while comfortably retaining edge space for status indicator strips.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** Standardized at `12px`–`14px` border-radius, maintaining structural harmony with larger containers.
- **Pills & Status Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create an immediate shape distinction between interactive tap targets and purely visual status badges.
- **Center Action Button:** Completely circular (`50%`) with elevated perimeter cushioning.

## Components

### 1. Modern Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Structure:** Floating white dock anchored to bottom edge with top-rounded lip (`24px`), height `72px`, bounded by a subtle top border (`#E3E8E3`).
- **Slots:** 4 tabs (e.g., Görevler, Ekip, Raporlar, Profil) flanking a centered recess.
- **Center Elevated Action Button:**
  - Size: `56x56px` circular button elevated `18px` above the navigation bar line.
  - Background: Solid Primary Green (`#3D8A4E`) or Dark Green gradient (`#2F5233` to `#3D8A4E`).
  - Icon: Prominent plus/lightning symbol (`#FFFFFF`), cast with a soft green ambient halo shadow.
  - Role: Rapid task creation, instant dispatch or emergency incident logging.
- **Item States:**
  - Inactive: `#8B9A8E` with `11px` label.
  - Active: `#3D8A4E` with `11px` bold label and a centered `4px` indicator dot beneath.

### 2. Left Hamburger Drawer
- **Header:** Deep green botanical gradient (`#2F5233` to `#1E3621`) spanning `220px` height.
  - Profile Avatar: `56px` circular avatar with a clean `2px` white border.
  - User Details: "Mehmet Demir", label "Ekip Lideri (Şef)", accompanied by a small active green badge.
- **Menu Items (7-Item Roster):**
  1. Günlük Operasyon Planı
  2. Ekip Yetkilendirme & Takip
  3. Kimyasal & Ekipman Stoğu
  4. Müşteri & Lokasyon Dizini
  5. Acil Müdahale Kayıtları
  6. Performans ve Kalite Raporu
  7. Sistem & Cihaz Ayarları
- **List Interaction:** `14px` Medium typography, `#16211A`. Selected state receives `#F1F8F2` background with `#3D8A4E` icon and a vertical left indicator strip.

### 3. Task Cards (Görev Kartları)
- **Geometry:** `18px` border radius, `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E3E8E3`.
- **Status Strip:** Left-aligned `5px` vertical bar colored semantically (Green for Active, Amber for Scheduled, Red for Urgent).
- **Content Organization:**
  - Top row: Pest type chip (e.g., "Kemirgen Kontrolü") + Time slot badge.
  - Center: Commercial client/site title (`16px` bold, `#16211A`) with address subtitle (`#5A6B5E`).
  - Footer row: Assigned technician avatar + **"Yeniden Ata" (Reassign)** quick action button (`#F1F8F2` background, `#3D8A4E` text, `12px` bold, `8px` rounded).

### 4. Stat Counter Cards (Sayaç Kartları)
- **Layout:** 2-column or 3-column horizontally scrollable or grid configuration.
- **Design:** Compact cards with `#FFFFFF` fill, `16px` rounded borders, and light padding (`12px 14px`).
- **Elements:** Micro-badge with semantic trend icon, large stat number (`24px` bold, `#16211A`), and micro caption label (`#5A6B5E`).

### 5. Team Workload Bars (Ekip İş Yükü Çubukları)
- **Container:** Dedicated team card with technician name, vehicle status, and assigned job ratio (e.g., "5/6").
- **Track & Fill:** `6px` tall progress track (`#E3E8E3`) with rounded pill ends. Fill color dynamically moves from `#3D8A4E` (optimal) to `#B57F13` (near capacity) and `#C0392B` (overloaded).

### 6. Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Height `24px`, padding `0 10px`, border radius `9999px`. Text is `11px` bold uppercase.
  - Success variant: `#F1F8F2` background, `#15803D` text.
  - Warning variant: `#FEF9EE` background, `#B57F13` text.
  - Danger variant: `#FDF2F2` background, `#C0392B` text.

### 7. Form Controls & Inputs
- **Inputs:** Height `48px`, `12px` rounded radius, `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E3E8E3`. Active focus swaps border to `2px solid #3D8A4E`. Placeholder text in `#8B9A8E`.
