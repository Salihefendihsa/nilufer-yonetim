## Brand & Style

The design system delivers an ergonomic, high-legibility mobile work companion specifically tailored for field pest-control technicians (specifically instantiated for field staff like Ali Kaya). Field operatives navigate high-stress, physically demanding environments—such as basements, commercial kitchens, manufacturing plants, and outdoor sites—often under glaring sunlight or low-light conditions, while wearing protective gear.

### Personality & Tone
- **Operational Clarity:** High information scent, rapid glanceability, zero visual clutter. Critical operational statuses (treatment logs, job manifests, biological target identification, emergency toxicity notes) take precedence over decorative flair.
- **Calm Authority:** Grounded, sanitary, and dependable. Green accents reinforce biosafety, organic balance, and systematic pest control without veering into playful consumer tones.
- **Tactile Ergonomics:** Exaggerated touch targets (minimum 48x48px), deliberate spacing for single-handed thumb interactions, and bold, recognizable visual indicators.

### Design Movement
- **Modern Tactical Utilitarianism:** Rooted in clean corporate utility blended with ambient tactile layers. It combines 1px structural low-contrast hairline borders (`#E3E8E3`), generous 16–18px surface radiuses, and crisp, contextual state indicators to create an interface that is functional, dependable, and visually pristine.

## Colors

The color system is engineered for outdoor contrast, clinical professionalism, and strict semantic clarity. The palette leverages earthy, biological greens balanced against neutral stone-green backgrounds and functional status tokens.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#3D8A4E`):** Core action buttons, verified job states, active navigation tabs, and primary interactive components.
- **Dark Green / Secondary (`#2F5233`):** High-prominence headers, key data metrics, selected operational states, and authoritative surface boundaries.
- **Light Green Tint / Tertiary (`#F1F8F2`):** Primary chip backgrounds, active card highlights, input field active containers, and subtle informational callouts.
- **App Background (`#F4F7F4`):** Soft, non-glare canvas that minimizes eye fatigue during extended outdoor/indoor shifts.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast, pure-white cards for operational tasks, client details, and form fields.
- **Border Stroke (`#E3E8E3`):** Crisp boundary definition across all containers, inputs, and list dividers.

### Typography Swatches
- **Text Primary (`#16211A`):** Deep charcoal-green ensuring AAA compliance on white and tint surfaces. Used for job titles, client names, and data readings.
- **Text Secondary (`#5A6B5E`):** Balanced mid-tone for addresses, timestamps, field notes, and contextual metadata.
- **Text Faint (`#8B9A8E`):** De-emphasized cues, placeholder text, inactive tab states, and unselected toggle labels.

### Semantic Tokens
- **Success (`#15803D`):** Completed treatments, confirmed signatures, applied certifications.
- **Warning (`#B57F13`):** Critical chemical hazard warnings, scheduled appointments running late, pending customer confirmations.
- **Danger (`#C0392B`):** Cancelled visits, active chemical exposure alerts, urgent biohazard notifications.
- **Info (`#1F6FA8`):** Navigation hints, customer special instructions, equipment tracking notes.

## Typography

The typography scale utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. Its geometric underpinnings combined with warm, humanist apertures ensure legibility on high-density mobile viewports and rapid scanning when mounted in vehicle docks or held in the field.

### Hierarchy & Application
- **Headline Large (24px/32px Bold):** Screen titles, staff greeting (`Merhaba, Ali Kaya`), and primary shift status summaries.
- **Headline Medium (20px/28px Bold):** Section banners, customer inspection location headers, and modal sheet titles.
- **Headline Small (18px/24px SemiBold):** Card-level titles, active job customer names, and chemical dosage labels.
- **Body Large (16px/24px Medium):** Standard form inputs, barcode scan readout numbers, and primary button labels.
- **Body Medium (14px/20px Regular):** Primary narrative content, inspection guidelines, customer notes, and address descriptions.
- **Body Small (13px/18px Regular):** Timestamps, historical logs, device serial numbers, and subsidiary instructions.
- **Label Large / Medium / Small (11–14px SemiBold):** Badges, chip tags, tabular metrics, and form field category trackers. Turkish diacritics (ç, ğ, ı, ö, ş, ü, İ) render with optical balance and consistent accent clearance.

## Layout & Spacing

This mobile design system relies on a single-column, thumb-accessible layout framework tailored for handheld smartphones (360px to 430px base viewport widths).

### Layout System
- **Viewport Safe Margins:** Base horizontal inset is fixed at `1rem` (16px) on mobile viewports, scaling to `1.25rem` (20px) on large handhelds.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 4px baseline sub-grid, with primary structural gaps operating on 8px multiples (`space-xs`: 8px, `space-md`: 16px, `space-xl`: 24px).
- **Card Spacing:** Work manifest items, chemical usage logs, and client detail modules maintain an internal padding of `card-padding` (18px) and a bottom separation of `space-md` (16px).
- **One-Handed Zone:** All primary action mechanisms (e.g., "Görevi Başlat", "Raporu Tamamla", "İmza Al") are pinned to sticky bottom action trays with safe area padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an ambient combination of structural low-contrast borders (`#E3E8E3`) and diffused botanical drop shadows, keeping surfaces crisp and legible in high ambient glare.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the base canvas (`#F4F7F4`) and inline recessed areas (e.g., historical timeline tracks, inactive input backgrounds).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Standard job card, appointment summary, and navigation headers. 
  - *Border:* `1px solid #E3E8E3`
  - *Shadow:* `0 2px 6px -1px rgba(22, 33, 26, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(22, 33, 26, 0.02)`
- **Level 2 (Active / Dragged / Interactive Card):** Tapped cards, currently running inspection logs, and floating counter indicators.
  - *Border:* `1px solid #3D8A4E` (tinted)
  - *Shadow:* `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(47, 82, 51, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(22, 33, 26, 0.04)`
- **Level 3 (Sticky Trays & Bottom Sheets):** Fixed bottom navigation bar, signature capture pads, and chemical selector bottom sheets.
  - *Border Top:* `1px solid #E3E8E3`
  - *Shadow:* `0 -4px 20px -2px rgba(22, 33, 26, 0.06)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces an approachable, modern physical presence through controlled, generous curvature designed to feel comfortable during repetitive field use.

### Radius Scale
- **Cards and Containers:** Fixed at `16px` to `18px` (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`). This accommodates nested sub-elements without visual tension.
- **Buttons and Input Controls:** Standardized at `12px` to `14px` for high-volume finger targets.
- **Pills, Chips, and Badges:** Fully circular (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for status indicators, Lucide-icon containers, and technician counters.
- **Dialogs & Bottom Sheets:** Upper corner radiuses of `24px` for natural bottom-up slide reveals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Uygulamayı Başlat", "İşlemi Tamamla"):** Background `#3D8A4E`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, height `48px` or `52px`, radius `12px`. Hover/Active state darkens to `#2F5233`. Accompanied by Lucide directional icons (18px, right-aligned).
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Müşteriyi Ara", "Yol Tarifi"):** Background `#F1F8F2`, foreground `#2F5233`, border `1px solid #3D8A4E` (30% opacity). Active state triggers full `#E3E8E3` background.
- **Destructive/Emergency (e.g., "Görevi İptal Et", "Acil Durum"):** Background `#FFFFFF`, foreground `#C0392B`, border `1px solid #C0392B`.

### Badges & Status Pills
- **Badge Anatomy:** Compact height (`26px` to `28px`), `rounded-full`, padding `4px 12px`, typography `label-md`.
- **Status Variants:**
  - *Tamamlandı (Completed):* Background `#F1F8F2`, text `#15803D`, border `1px solid #15803D/20`, icon `CheckCircle2`.
  - *Bekliyor / Yolda (In Progress / En Route):* Background `#FEF9C3`, text `#B57F13`, border `1px solid #B57F13/30`, icon `Clock`.
  - *İptal Edildi (Cancelled):* Background `#FDF2F2`, text `#C0392B`, border `1px solid #C0392B/20`, icon `AlertTriangle`.
  - *Planlandı (Scheduled):* Background `#F0F7FD`, text `#1F6FA8`, border `1px solid #1F6FA8/20`, icon `Calendar`.

### Cards (Task & Inspection Units)
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E3E8E3`, border-radius `18px`, padding `18px`.
- **Content Hierarchy:**
  - *Header Row:* Status Badge (left), Job ID (`#8B9A8E`, right).
  - *Middle Block:* Client / Business Name in `headline-sm` (`#16211A`), followed by address line with a Lucide `MapPin` icon in `body-sm` (`#5A6B5E`).
  - *Footer Row:* Scheduled timeframe chip, pest category pill (e.g., "Kemirgen Kontrol", "Jel İlaçlama"), and quick-action icon buttons (phone call, navigation).

### Input Fields & Inspection Forms
- **Standard Text / Numeric Input:** Container background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E3E8E3`, height `48px`, radius `12px`, text `body-lg` (`#16211A`).
- **Active / Focused State:** Border transitions to `2px solid #3D8A4E`, background shifts to `#FFFFFF`, no glowing rings.
- **Dosage & Quantity Steppers:** Dedicated left/right tactile increment buttons (`44x44px`, background `#F1F8F2`, text `#2F5233`) framing a centered numeric readout.

### Checkboxes, Radios, and Multi-Select Pests
- **Checkbox:** `22x22px`, radius `6px`. Unchecked: border `2px solid #E3E8E3`, background `#FFFFFF`. Checked: background `#3D8A4E`, border `#3D8A4E`, check icon `#FFFFFF`.
- **Radio Buttons:** `22x22px`, circular. Selected state features an inner `#3D8A4E` dot (`10px`).
- **Pest Target Grid Tiles:** 2-column or 3-column selectable cards featuring pest silhouette or Lucide pest icon, title label, and instant toggle state (selected: `#F1F8F2` background with `#3D8A4E` border).

### Field Staff Header Module (Ali Kaya Personal View)
- **Top Profile Bar:** Technician avatar with status dot (online/on-field), staff name ("Ali Kaya"), role badge ("Kıdemli Saha Teknisyeni"), and quick shift toggle button ("Görevde").
- **Daily Progress Bar:** Dual-tone progress track displaying completed vs. remaining visits for the current route.
